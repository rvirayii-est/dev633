## What it is

A group of web applications built on the MERN stack, developed and managed as an
ongoing engagement rather than a single delivery.

## Why it is here

Most of my work is typed, compiled and relational: Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL. This
engagement was the opposite on every axis, which made the trade-offs unusually visible.

Document storage removes the schema migration step, and that genuinely speeds up early
iteration. It does not remove the schema. It moves it into the application code, where
nothing enforces it and every reader has to defend against shapes the writer stopped
producing months ago.

## My role

Developed and managed the applications across the stack, from the data layer through
the Express services to the React interfaces.
